# **App Name**: Harpa Studio

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Portfolio Showcase: Categorized display of creative projects and artistic work (Posters, Album Covers, Motion Posters, etc.), enabling visitors to explore Harpa's diverse body of work.
- Creative Lab & Experiments Gallery: Dedicated section to present experimental visuals, typography studies, and AI-generated concepts from the studio's research and development.
- Harpa Journal & Insights Blog: Publish and display curated articles, design notes, creative process breakdowns, and studio updates, offering visitors deeper insights.
- AI Creative Assistant Chatbot: An interactive chatbot tool, powered by the Groq API, designed to provide design feedback, generate creative concepts, offer 'Smart Critiques,' or answer studio-related inquiries.
- Inquiry & Collaboration Forms: Provide streamlined forms for visitors to easily contact Harpa for commissions, collaborations, press inquiries, or general questions.
- 'Join the Lab' Application: A pathway for aspiring creatives to learn more about and apply to join the Harpa collective or community, as highlighted in the Hero section.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color Anchor: The palette evokes muted elegance and sophisticated artistry, drawing inspiration from cinematic and contemporary design moods. The color scheme is dark-themed.
- Primary color: A desaturated, warm beige (#DEDBC8) to serve as a bright accent on the dark background for interactive elements and highlighted text. HSL(68, 10%, 83%).
- Background color: A deep, muted forest green-black (#171814) provides a sophisticated and immersive canvas for content. HSL(68, 15%, 8%).
- Accent color: A rich, warm sepia (#806146) used sparingly to draw attention to key elements and create visual depth. HSL(38, 45%, 50%).
- Headlines and emphasis text: 'Instrument Serif' (serif), chosen for its elegant and artistic character. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body text and general content: 'Almarai' (sans-serif), providing a clean, modern, and highly legible foundation. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Utilize simple, modern outline icons, consistent with the minimalist aesthetic. Integration with Lucide icons is recommended for versatility and crispness.
- Responsive grid-based design ensures optimal viewing across devices. Features include ample white space, centralized content sections with defined max-widths, and clear section separation for an organized feel.
- Implement subtle and elegant animations, such as word-by-word text reveals, smooth fade-ins with slight vertical translations, and gentle scaling effects on interactive elements. All animations should be smooth and deliberate, leveraging Framer Motion principles for a polished user experience.
