Capabilities

What we do & how we work.

Editorial Design

Catalogs, magazines, books, printed journals. Grid systems that breathe. Typography as primary visual language.

Poster & Print

Campaigns, art prints, exhibition work. Paper stock selection as design decision. Letterpress, risograph, offset, digital.

Art Direction

Full-scope identity systems. Logo to laundry tag. Consistency across 50+ touchpoints without templates.

Type Customization

Variable font exploitation. Kern tables tuned per project. Custom weights and optical sizes for specific contexts.

The Studio

Craft, collaboration,
production detail.

Harpa is a small editorial practice between Lisbon and New York. We work with cultural institutions, independent fashion houses, and record labels—clients who care about craft. Each year we take on 12–16 projects maximum. We believe design is inseparable from production. Knowing your printer, understanding paper, respecting ink—these are creative decisions.
