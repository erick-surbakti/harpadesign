About

Craft & intention.

Harpa Design Studio is a creative practice focused on strategic branding and visual direction. We work with ambitious brands and institutions to create meaningful visual communication that drives impact.

Our approach combines strategic thinking with meticulous craft. We specialize in brand identity systems, digital design, and comprehensive visual solutions that evolve with your growth.

Based in Jakarta, we collaborate with leading companies across Southeast Asia and beyond. We believe in creating work that matters—designs that communicate clearly, inspire action, and build lasting connections.
