About The Studio

Crafted with precision & purpose.

Harpa Design Studio is a creative practice dedicated to transforming brand visions into powerful visual experiences. We specialize in strategic branding, design systems, and comprehensive visual direction for ambitious organizations.

Our approach combines strategic thinking with meticulous craft. We don't just create designs—we build comprehensive systems that evolve with your brand's growth. From initial concept to full-scale implementation, we ensure every detail serves your brand's purpose.

Based in Jakarta, Indonesia, we work with leading financial institutions, corporate brands, and innovative startups across Southeast Asia and beyond. We believe in creating work that matters—designs that communicate clearly, inspire action, and build lasting connections.

Every project reflects our commitment to excellence: thoughtful strategy, meticulous typography, purposeful color systems, and beautiful execution across all applications.
