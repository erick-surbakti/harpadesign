About

Craft, collaboration,
production detail.

Harpa is a small editorial practice between Lisbon and New York. We work with cultural institutions, independent publishers, and forward-thinking brands—clients who believe design is a material practice. Each year we take 12–16 projects maximum. We believe in knowing your printer, understanding paper, respecting ink. These aren't afterthoughts—they're creative decisions.
